**Description**

Shop Your Values. Vote With Your Wallet.

The Little Blue Cart app is for discovering and shopping small businesses that share your values, all in one place. Search a vetted directory of progressive businesses, buy directly from independent makers, and connect with a community that cares where its money goes.

Every dollar you spend is a vote. Now it's easy to cast one.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Search with confidence: Find businesses by product, service, or identity. Every one is vetted, so you know who you're supporting.
• Shop in the same app: Found something you love? Buy it right here from the progressive sellers on Little Blue Market.
• Meet the makers: Get to know the people and stories behind what you buy.
• Join the community: Share your finds, swap recommendations, and connect with other shoppers and business owners who get it.
• Skip the big guys: Put your money into businesses that reflect what you believe.

WHY LITTLE BLUE CART APP
Shopping with intention shouldn't mean hours of research or a guilt spiral. We do the vetting. You do the fun part. It's hopeful, practical, and a little bit joyful, because small actions add up.

FOR BUSINESS OWNERS
Run a progressive small business? Get discovered by shoppers looking for exactly what you offer.

Download Little Blue Cart and start shopping like it matters.
